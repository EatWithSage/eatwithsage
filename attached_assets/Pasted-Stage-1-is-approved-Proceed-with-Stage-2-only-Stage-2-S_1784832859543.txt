## How Sage Works

Add `id="how-sage-works"` to the section.

After that ID exists, update the hero’s “See How It Works” CTA so it points to `#how-sage-works` instead of `#retail-media-opportunity`.

Use this section structure:

Eyebrow:

How Sage Works

Headline:

From Weekly Food Decisions to Measurable Commerce

Intro:

Sage connects a useful shopper experience with ecommerce and new media opportunities—all under the retailer’s brand.

### Personalized

Sage creates weekly food recommendations around each household’s preferences, dietary needs, schedule and shopping behavior.

### Shoppable

Recommendations are connected to the grocer’s assortment and transformed into a complete, editable ecommerce basket.

### Monetizable

Relevant products and brands can be integrated into the experience, creating new retailer-controlled media opportunities close to the point of purchase.

Do not add performance metrics, attribution claims or assertions that Sage has already produced measurable lift.

## Ecommerce Engine

Use this section structure:

Eyebrow:

Ecommerce Engine

Headline:

Media That Helps Build the Basket

Intro:

Most digital advertising ends with a click. Sage is designed to connect personalized food recommendations, relevant product discovery and the retailer’s ecommerce experience.

Supporting steps:

1. Understand household needs
2. Recommend a personalized week
3. Match ingredients to the retailer’s assortment
4. Let shoppers review or replace products
5. Continue into the retailer’s existing ecommerce journey

Featured statement:

Sage does not simply send shoppers to ecommerce. It helps create the basket.

The phrase “helps create the basket” is intentional. Do not change it to an absolute claim such as “creates the basket” or “increases basket size.”
